## Introduction
The **VR Table Tennis Multiplayer Game** is a highly immersive virtual reality game that brings the thrill of table tennis to VR. Designed for both casual and competitive players, this game features real-time multiplayer functionality powered by **Photon Fusion PUN2**, ensuring seamless connectivity and gameplay. Advanced custom physics predictions minimize the effects of lag, providing players with an authentic table tennis experience. Built with **Unity**, **OpenXR**, and **C#**, the game is cross-platform compatible and designed to deliver a realistic and competitive VR sports experience.

## Features
- **Real-Time Multiplayer:** Play with friends or compete with players around the world in a seamless multiplayer environment.
- **Custom Physics Predictions:** Ensures realistic ball trajectories and smooth gameplay by mitigating lag effects.
- **Immersive Gameplay:** Realistic table tennis physics, paddle control, and ball behavior for a lifelike experience.
- **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Used OpenXR for releasing on multiple platforms in the future.

## Benefits
- **Competitive Fun:** Enjoy real-time matches against friends or global opponents.
- **Skill Building:** Sharpen your table tennis skills in a realistic virtual environment.
- **Low Latency Gameplay:** Experience smooth gameplay with advanced physics predictions for lag compensation.
- **Global Community:** Connect with table tennis enthusiasts from around the world.

## Usage
1. **Setup and Installation:**
   - Download and install the game on your VR headset via side loading.
   - Open the app and grant necessary permissions.

2. **Starting the Game:**
   - Create or join a multiplayer room using the in-game menu.
   - Wait for an opponent to join or invite a friend to play.

3. **Gameplay:**
   - Use your VR controllers to hit the ball and play table tennis.
   - Adjust gameplay settings as needed for paddle sensitivity or ball speed.
   - Win matches by reaching the required score before your opponent.


## Tools Used
- **Unity:** Game development engine for creating immersive VR environments and interactions.
- **Photon Fusion PUN2:** Multiplayer framework for real-time connectivity and room management.
- **OpenXR:** Ensures compatibility across VR platforms.
- **C#:** Programming language used for scripting and game logic.
- **Custom Physics Predictions:** To ensure realistic ball trajectories and mitigate lag effects during online play.

## Requirements
- **VR Headset:** Any OpenXR-compatible VR headset (e.g., Meta Quest, HTC Vive, Valve Index).
- **Unity Version:** Unity 2022.3 or higher for development purposes.
- **Internet Connection:** Stable connection required for multiplayer gameplay.
- **Platform Requirements:** 
  - **PC:** Windows 10 or higher
  - **Standalone VR:** Meta Quest 2 or higher
